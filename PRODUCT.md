# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Anyone can explore and contribute, on desktop or mobile. Waterloo is the first neighborhood, not an admission rule.

## Product Purpose

Memory Box is a large continuous shared canvas containing many different places. People discover individual stories inside a collective creation and add pixels, notes, photos, ideas, jokes and work in progress. Success means seeing a place worth visiting, finding an original worth reading, and having a visible way to contribute.

## Operating Context

People travel at world, neighborhood and close distances. They can wander without a query, paint together with real presence, leave authored originals, respond beside another original, and invite others to help make something. Search and cited Ask provide another entrance into the spatial world.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing app has shared painting, 15-second paint turns, undo, live cursors, text/image/link originals, build-on relationships, open build invitations, exact-spot sharing, hybrid search and source-backed answers. The sparse write plane runs from -60000 to 59999 world units. The pilot still fetches full snapshots; this is not evidence of million-user capacity. Preserve ownership, drafts, moderation, reduced motion, touch targets and the authority of stored content. Do not move others' originals or fabricate people, contributions, relationships or activity.

## Brand Commitments

Memory Box. White/pastel, cute pixel art, compact readable controls, a canvas-first experience. r/place, Kinopio and Wplace are user-named inspirations. Keep copy direct and free of decorative slogans. Existing fonts, icons and materials are documented in `.21st/DESIGN.md`.

## Evidence on Hand

Stored community contributions and explicitly fictional starter originals/pixel landscapes. Existing browser fixtures exercise real hosted integration with disposable identities; they are not adoption evidence.

## Product Principles

- Discovery should reveal the scale of what people make and leave together.
- Places can develop their own character with soft boundaries; themes are invitations, not required categories.
- Individual words, photos, authorship and contributions stay accessible inside the larger creation.
- Structure supports travel and collaboration without turning the world into a filing cabinet.
- Search brings people to originals and places; generated answers retain their sources.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable controls, keyboard operation, visible focus, mobile touch targets, reduced motion and clear disconnected/error states remain required.
